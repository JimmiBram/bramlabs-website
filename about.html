Jimmi Bram — Bram Labs Founder

Jimmi Bram

Technical Architect & Builder

Jimmi Bram is a Technical Architect & Builder working at the intersection of AI, software architecture, and real-world implementation.

With more than 20 years of experience in software development and technical leadership — including roles such as CTO at Alm. Brand and engineering leadership at Saxo Bank — he focuses on turning ideas into working systems that create value early.

Jimmi's approach is pragmatic. He builds end-to-end systems early to prove viability and expose the real challenges of a problem. The goal is not perfect architecture from day one, but the smallest system that can demonstrate value while still leaving a clear path for the system to evolve.

He works hands-on across the stack — coding, prototyping, architecting, and configuring systems until they become something useful in the real world.
