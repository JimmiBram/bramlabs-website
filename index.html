Philosophy

AI can build everything. That is not the same as knowing what to build.

For most of software’s history the constraint was building. Teams were sized by how much code had to be written, and roadmaps were sorted by what was cheap to make. That constraint is gone. A model with a terminal writes, tests, and ships most of what a product needs, and the cost of a feature is falling toward the cost of describing it precisely.

When building is close to free, a product is decided by what you ask for and by what you accept. Knowing what to ask for is domain knowledge. Knowing what to accept is taste. Knowing what happens after launch, when a customer churns or an invoice fails or a teacher opens it in front of thirty students, is experience. Those three are what a model does not have, and they are what BramLabs is built around.

The model does

  • Writes the code
  • Writes the tests
  • Drafts the copy
  • Wires the integrations
  • Reads the logs
  • Ships the release

A person decides

  • Which problem is worth a product
  • What the product is not
  • When it is good enough
  • What it costs and what it charges
  • What to do when it breaks
  • Whether to keep going

How we work

Small by design

  1. 01

    A product starts around taste

    Every product here begins with someone who knows the domain and can tell good from merely finished. AI does the building. The team stays small while the product finds its users. Traction earns the organisation around it, not the other way round.

  2. 02

    Whole system first, then depth

    We build end to end early and thin, so the real problems show up in week one instead of month six. The target is the smallest system that proves value and leaves a clear path to grow.

  3. 03

    Real users, early

    A product is an argument, and users are the only ones who get to say whether it holds. We ship to a first group fast and let what they actually do set the roadmap.

  4. 04

    AI is the workshop, not the pitch

    Every product here is built with AI, and most use it inside. Neither is the point. The point is the problem it solves, and that is how we describe each one.

  5. 05

    It leaves, or it ends

    A product that grows is shed off from the studio to become something big on its own, with its own company and its own people. A product that stops growing is closed down, cleanly, and what it taught us goes into the next one. The studio keeps neither out of sentiment.
